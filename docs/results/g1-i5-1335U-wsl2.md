# G1 results: i5-1335U under WSL2

Both G1 gates pass on this machine at commit `afbd85b`: speed on all 10 real and 6 generated matrices, and accuracy on all 10 real matrices with Jacobi, on the 6 smaller real matrices with plain CG, and on the 6 generated matrices. **G1 is not closed yet:** no other team machine has run. All runs used b = A·random, on AC power, pinned to logical CPU 3, with no pauses recorded.

The tables below are the output of `scripts/summarize.py` on the CSV files in `results/i5-1335U-wsl2/`. Ratios below 1 mean faster than Eigen.

## What this does not show

- **One machine.** G1 requires every team machine. macOS and Clang builds are untested.
- **WSL2 is not ideal for reference numbers.** It virtualizes the CPU layout, and a pinned vCPU can move between performance and efficiency cores. Many intervals are wider than 0.10, as the warnings above say, so differences of a few percent are not resolved.
- **Single-round rows are not paired.** Solve times with 1 round show one sample each.
- **Kernel results** are in `g1-kernels.csv` and `g1-real-kernels.csv`. Print them with `--kernel-baseline eigen`.
